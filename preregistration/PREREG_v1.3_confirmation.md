# Pre-registration v1.3: confirmation layers and statistical reporting

Written 2026-10-08, tag `prereg-v1.3`. Adds confirmation analyses and reporting standards. Nothing earlier is changed.
State of knowledge when written: ME/CFS cluster-level results (models A and B, groups, HPA) exist on disk but have NOT
been opened; the control traits were still running. Gene-level ME/CFS results were seen as a QC check only.

## Why
A single method on a single GWAS is not enough to call a result confirmed. These layers test the main finding with a
different statistical model, a different null, deliberately damaged data, an independent GWAS, and independent
literature. A result is only called "confirmed" if it survives most of them.

## C1 Different method: partitioned heritability (S-LDSC, own implementation)
- Annotation per cluster: SNPs within 100 kb of the cluster's top 10% specific genes (gene sets already built,
  `top10_sets.txt`). Control annotations in the same model: all SNPs, and all expressed genes +/- 100 kb.
- Regression of chi-square on the LD scores of the three annotations, HapMap3 regression SNPs, LD computed from the
  1000G EUR reference, 200-block jackknife, one-sided test of the cluster coefficient > 0.
- Agreement is measured two ways: Spearman correlation of the 461 cluster z-values between MAGMA and S-LDSC, and the
  share of MAGMA FDR-significant clusters with S-LDSC p < 0.05.
- The same code must reproduce the control biology (schizophrenia in excitatory neurons, Alzheimer's in microglia).

## C2 Different null: permutation of gene-level statistics
- For the 20 clusters with the smallest model-A p, shuffle the gene-level z within bins of gene size and mean expression
  (5 x 5 bins), 5,000 times, and recompute the gene-property statistic. Empirical p < 0.05 counts as confirmed.

## C3 Damaged data: is it one locus or the whole genome?
- Locus drop: remove every gene within 1 Mb of any variant with p < 5e-8 in the QC'd gwas_1, rerun model A.
- Leave one chromosome out: remove each autosome in turn (22 reruns), rerun model A.
- A cluster is robust if its p stays below 0.05 after the locus drop and in at least 20 of 22 leave-one-chromosome-out runs.
  Spearman correlation of the 461-cluster profile between full and damaged runs is reported.

## C4 Independent GWAS
- `decodeme_gwas_2` (same cases, different control subset) and, if its heritability z >= 4, the UK Biobank-coded chronic
  fatigue syndrome GWAS (GCST90038694; about 2,000 cases, overlapping controls).
- Concordance: Spearman correlation of the 461-cluster z-profile and the share of gwas_1 significant clusters nominally
  significant in the other GWAS in the same direction.

## E1 Convergence with independent literature
- Region set fixed from published imaging and physiology on ME/CFS (searches of 2026-10-08): basal nuclei (putamen),
  thalamus, midbrain, pons, medulla (brainstem), hippocampus, amygdala, hypothalamus. Sources: PET neuroinflammation
  (11C-PK11195 2014; TSPO-PET putamen), brainstem/hypothalamic MRI and perfusion, orexin/hypothalamus reviews.
- Statistic: mean cell-weighted region score in the set minus the mean outside it. Null: the same statistic for every panel
  trait. ME/CFS counts as converging only if it exceeds the 90th percentile of the panel and a permutation of region labels
  (10,000) gives p < 0.05. Otherwise "no convergence beyond what brain traits generally show".

## E2 Statistical reporting standards (always produced)
- Genomic inflation (lambda GC) and quantile-quantile plot for gene-level and cluster-level p-values.
- Pipeline false-positive check: number of FDR-significant clusters for the non-neural negative control (height) and for
  ME/CFS with case labels not available, so instead for a gene-level z permutation of ME/CFS (10 draws).
- Effect sizes with 95% CI (MAGMA beta, SE) for every reported cluster; heritability z, mean chi-square and
  LDSC intercept for every GWAS; sample sizes; all tests and exclusions listed in an evidence ledger
  (`results/EVIDENCE_LEDGER.md`, generated by script).

## Rule for the word "confirmed"
A cluster, group or pathway is called confirmed only if it passes its main test (PREREG v1 or v1.2) and at least two of
C1, C2, C3, C4. One of the four is "partly supported". None is "not confirmed".

## Not allowed
Adding or dropping confirmation tests, changing the region set in E1, or changing the pass rules after results are seen.
