## ME/CFS genetics (the core)
- DecodeME preprint, "Initial findings from the DecodeME genome-wide association study of
  ME/CFS" — https://www.medrxiv.org/content/10.1101/2025.08.06.25333109v1.full  (PDF read in full
  from the Edinburgh-hosted copy.) Key facts: 15,579 cases (84% female), 259,909 UKB controls
  (GWAS-1); six loci in GWAS-1, eight total across GWAS-1/GWAS-2/infection/female; LDSC liability
  h2 = 0.095 (SD 0.006); REGENIE; FUMA v1.8.0; MAGMA gene-based: 13 genes significant of 18,637;
  MAGMA gene-tissue: all 13 brain tissues significant (p < 0.05/54); coloc of CA10 locus with an
  eQTL; replication cohorts R-1 (13,767 cases / 212,183 controls) and R-2 (15,251 cases /
  1,878,066 controls); UK Biobank case-replication GWAS "to be provided". Sex bias in associations;
  ARFGEF2 and CA10 signals found in females replicated in males.
- DecodeME researcher access / summary statistics — https://institute-genetics-cancer.ed.ac.uk/decodeme-the-worlds-largest-mecfs-study/researcher-access
  OSF: https://osf.io/rgqs3/files/osfstorage  (individual-level data needs a Data Access Committee
  application; summary statistics do not).
- Informal forum analysis (NOT peer reviewed): "Using MAGMA on ME/CFS genetic data", S4ME —
  https://s4me.info/threads/using-magma-on-me-cfs-genetic-data.50465/  (FUMA cell-type on Siletti
  datasets, 3,172 tests, 24 significant, all neurons). This means plain cell-type mapping is NOT novel.
- Third-party reanalyses: mitochondria/gene-expression + repurposing (Realomics-type) —
  https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12940889/ ; PrecisionLife combinatorial analysis, >40
  core candidate targets, TLR3/rintatolimod section —
  https://link.springer.com/article/10.1186/s12967-026-08167-1 ; Million Veteran Program +
  DecodeME meta-analysis (19,470 cases / 699,111 controls; reported in search results);
  genetic correlation + MR study using DecodeME summary stats (76/180 long-COVID genes also
  associated with ME).
- Long COVID multi-ancestry GWAS (23andMe), links to chronic fatigue, fibromyalgia, depression —
  https://www.medrxiv.org/content/10.1101/2024.10.07.24315052v1
- Fibromyalgia GWAS 2026 — https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-026-75256-6
